# Adidas US Sales Analysis Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview
This project analyzes the **Adidas US Sales Dataset (2020–2021)** to uncover key business insights related to sales performance, profitability, product performance, regional trends, and sales methods. The analysis was performed using **SQL** for data exploration and querying, **Power BI** for interactive dashboard creation, and **Python** for exploratory data analysis (EDA).

---

## 🎯 Business Objective
To help Adidas stakeholders answer key business questions such as:
- Which regions generate the highest sales and profit?
- Which products contribute the most revenue?
- Which retailers are the top performers?
- How do monthly sales change over time?
- Which sales method performs the best?
- What actionable insights can improve business performance?

## 📈 Key Business Insights

- West Region generated the highest sales among all regions.
- Men's Street Footwear was the highest revenue-generating product.
- California and New York were among the top-performing states.
- Online sales outperformed Outlet and In-store sales methods.
- Sales showed noticeable fluctuations across months, indicating seasonal demand patterns.
